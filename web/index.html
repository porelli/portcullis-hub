Doctor

What the Hub finds wrong or risky in the apps' folders, ports and settings. The doctor never changes anything by itself: every fix is previewed first and applied only when you click Apply.

Every change made on the Hub and on the people page (people, groups, app settings, AI), newest first. Pick "via AI" to see only the changes made through the Portcullis MCP, or add the AI requests from the access log.

Sign-in

Which sign-in methods people may use on this server, and templates: named rules over those methods, each with a score out of 5. One template is the default; each app picks the default or another enabled template in its Settings tab, and Portcullis's own pages are set below. Wherever a template applies, the people who can't meet it yet are listed.

AI support

People's AI assistants (Claude, Claude Code, other MCP clients) can use their apps as them, through core's mcp-gate: each app's AI support tab switches it on for that app. Model providers are where the AI page apps send what people type there.

Domains

Extra domains or subdomains for app instances (never for the portal, Hub, people or sign-in pages). Portcullis points <domain> and *.<domain> at this server and obtains a wildcard website certificate (HTTPS) with the DNS provider's API token. A domain receives hostnames once its records are OK and its website certificate is valid. Tokens are write-only: they go to core's domains service (encrypted at rest) and are never shown again.

Shared folders

Host folders several apps use. Each folder gets its own group: its files and folders are given that group, setgid and a default ACL, so whatever one app writes the others can read (or write). Assigning apps is always previewed first, a permissions snapshot is saved before anything changes, and the last apply can be undone. Removing a folder never deletes files.

Local networks

Your LAN and VPN ranges. The doctor judges LAN-only ports (file sharing, discovery) against them, and "Rebind…" only accepts an address inside them.
